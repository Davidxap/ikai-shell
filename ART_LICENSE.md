## What this is

Fan art. The engraved icon style, the marks, the wallpapers and the seals are all original work made
for this project, but they draw directly on the visual language of Junji Ito's manga (ink, bone,
blood, spirals, the recurring faces) and, in a few marks, on Silent Hill. The bar's shapes (islands,
pills, the notch) also take some aesthetic cues from Shibumi-Shell's own look, on top of the code it
was forked from (see `plugins/ito.bar/NOTICE` for that, under MIT). IKAI has no affiliation
with Junji Ito, his publishers, Konami, HANCORE, or any other rights holder, and is not endorsed by
any of them.
